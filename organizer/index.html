What you get

Everything you need to run ticketing

Event pages that sell

Upload a poster, describe the event, set venue and times, and publish. Your page is fast, mobile-first and search friendly.

Flexible ticket types

Early Bird, Regular, VIP, VVIP or your own naming. Control price, quantity and sales windows per ticket type.

Secure online payments

Customers pay through Paystack. Card and mobile money options are shown on the Paystack checkout.

Digital tickets & QR codes

Each paid ticket carries a unique code. Staff scan at the gate and the server confirms validity instantly.

Orders and attendees

Filter orders by status, search attendees, and export your attendee list to CSV for your records.

Gate check-in

Give staff access to a single event check-in tool with a manual lookup fallback when a phone is cracked or offline.

Getting started

Live in three steps

1

Create your organizer account

Register with your name, email and phone, then add your business details in organizer settings.

2

Build your event

Add the poster, venue, date and description, then create your ticket types with prices and quantities.

3

Submit for approval

Switch the event to active - that submits it to the platform team, who review it and approve it before it goes on sale.

4

Publish and sell

Once approved the event is live. Customers can now find it, buy tickets and receive digital tickets.

Payments

Money handling you can trust

  • Ticket prices are calculated and locked on our servers, not in the browser.
  • An order is only marked paid after Paystack confirms the payment to our Worker.
  • Every ticket is generated after payment confirmation, never before.
  • Settlement details and platform fees are agreed with each organizer before going live.

Day of event

Smooth entry at the gate

  • Event staff accounts are limited to the events you assign.
  • Scanning a ticket checks payment status, event status and prior check-in.
  • A used ticket shows the original check-in time, so duplicates are caught.
  • Print or share a manual lookup list for the entrance as a fallback.
