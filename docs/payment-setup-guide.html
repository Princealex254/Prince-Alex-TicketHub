Payment setup guide

Two ways to get paid for tickets: connect your own merchant account (Paystack, Pesapal or PayHero), or let buyers pay the platform and take your share. This guide lists exactly what to copy from each provider and where to paste it.

1What you do in TicketHub (same for every provider)

  1. Open Settings → Payment settings (tickethub.princealex.digital/organizer-settings/#payment). Save your organizer profile first if you have not – payment credentials attach to it.
  2. On the provider card (Paystack, Pesapal or PayHero) paste the values listed below, then press Save details.
  3. Press Test connection. It must pass before the provider can be used.
  4. Press Make active. That is all – one connected provider is enough to go live. Buyers never choose a gateway; every event without its own setting is sold through your Active Payment Provider.

2Option A – use your own merchant account

Pick whichever provider you already bank with. Card fees, payment methods and settlement timelines follow that provider's merchant terms.

PS

Paystack – cards, M-Pesa and more

Website: paystack.com · Dashboard: dashboard.paystack.com

  • Paystack public key (pk_live_...) – Paystack dashboard → Settings → API Keys & Webhooks.
  • Paystack secret key (sk_live_...) – the same page. It is entered once and encrypted; TicketHub never shows it again.

Use the Live keys (toggle at the top of the Paystack dashboard) when you are ready to sell for real; Test keys are only for Paystack's test mode.

PE

Pesapal – M-Pesa, cards and mobile money

Website: pesapal.com · Developer docs: developer.pesapal.com

  • Pesapal consumer key and consumer secret – sent to the email address on your Pesapal merchant account when the account is opened. Lost them? Log in to your Pesapal merchant dashboard and scroll to the bottom to have the details re-sent to that same email.

You do not need to create an IPN: TicketHub registers its payment callback with Pesapal for you.

Pesapal keys are environment-specific (sandbox or live). If Pesapal rejects your keys, ask platform support which environment this deployment uses.

PH

PayHero – M-Pesa STK push

Website: payhero.co.ke · Sign up / dashboard: app.payhero.co.ke · Docs: docs.payhero.co.ke

  • PayHero API username and API password – PayHero account → Settings → API (the password is hidden; reveal or copy it from that page).
  • PayHero channel id – Payment Channels → My Payment Channels: the numeric id of the M-Pesa paybill or till that receives the money (e.g. 133).

New to PayHero? Sign up, then create a channel (M-PESA STK Push, your paybill or till). PayHero approves accounts after KYC – usually within one business day – and your channel must be Live to receive real payments.

3Option B – platform payment (owner payment mode)

No merchant account of your own? In Payment settings, set Default: collect through the platform owner account to On. Buyers then pay into the platform's merchant account at checkout and your share is paid out to you by the platform. You can override this per event while creating or editing it (payment mode: owner account or your own). Nothing to paste, nothing to test.

If something is not working

  • Make active is greyed out – the provider is not connected yet, or its test has not passed.
  • Test connection fails – re-copy the values from the provider (check for stray spaces) and confirm the environment (Paystack Test/Live, Pesapal sandbox/live).
  • Card shows "not connected" – press Save details first, then test.
  • Checkout is closed – no provider is connected/active. Connect one, or switch the event to platform payment.

How your keys are protected

  • Credentials are encrypted (AES-GCM) by the Worker before they reach the database and are never sent back to the page – secret fields are write-only.
  • Buyers pay on the provider's own checkout; card and M-Pesa details are never stored on TicketHub.
  • Payment status is confirmed server-to-server with the provider before a ticket is issued.
