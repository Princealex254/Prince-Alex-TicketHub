How money moves

Your Event. Your Payment Provider.

Prince Alex TicketHub provides the event and ticketing platform, while the selected payment provider processes customer payments and settles funds according to its merchant terms.

Payment safety

Verified before tickets are issued

  • TicketHub verifies successful payment before issuing tickets.
  • An order is only confirmed after the payment provider notifies our server.
  • Every ticket is generated after payment confirmation, never before.
  • No payment credentials are stored on TicketHub.

Payments in detail

How Fees, Payouts and Refunds Work

The money side of selling tickets, in plain terms. All tickets are priced and paid in Kenyan Shillings.

Fees

Your TicketHub platform fee is agreed with you before your event goes live, and your payment provider charges its own processing fee under its merchant terms. Buyers pay exactly your ticket price — no service fee is added at checkout.

Getting paid

With your own merchant account, your provider settles takings on its normal schedule. In owner payment mode the platform collects the payment and you receive your share through the platform's payouts.

What buyers pay with

M-Pesa, cards and other mobile money options, depending on the provider connected to the event. M-Pesa payments arrive as a prompt on the buyer's phone and are confirmed by the provider before tickets are issued.

Refunds

Refunds follow the policy published on your event page and are processed through your provider's dashboard. Refunded orders are marked refunded and their tickets stop scanning at the gate.

Unpaid and free orders

An unpaid order holds its tickets for 30 minutes, then releases them for other buyers. Free tickets never touch the payment provider — the order is confirmed straight away.

Payment security

Ticket prices are recalculated on our servers, payment status comes only from the provider's confirmation, and no card or wallet details are ever stored on TicketHub.

Before you go live: Your platform fee and settlement arrangement are agreed with you directly, so there are no surprises after your first sale.
