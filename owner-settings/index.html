Owner payment settings collect on behalf of organizers

Active provider-

Connect the platform's own merchant accounts here. Organizers can then choose to collect through the owner's account (owner payment mode) for their events. Only the active owner provider is used, and buyers never see or choose a gateway. Credentials are encrypted (AES-GCM) by the Worker and are never sent back to this page.

Turning this off immediately closes owner payment mode for new checkouts. Existing paid orders and issued tickets are unchanged.

Every event in owner payment mode is sold through this provider.

Worker secrets (PAYSTACK_SECRET_KEY, PESAPAL_CONSUMER_KEY, PAYHERO_API_USERNAME) still act as a fallback when no owner account is saved here.
