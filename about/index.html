About us

Building the ticketing layer for Kenya’s events

Prince Alex TicketHub is a modern event ticketing platform by Prince Alex Digital. It gives organizers the tools to publish events, sell tickets online and manage attendance, while giving audiences a simple, secure way to buy and use tickets.

For event organizers

Create an event, add ticket types, upload a poster and start selling in minutes. Orders, attendees and check-ins stay in one dashboard.

For ticket buyers

Search events by category, date, location or price, pay securely and receive a digital ticket with a QR code for entry.

For event staff

Scan tickets at the gate with a phone. Our servers validate every ticket, so a used or invalid ticket cannot slip through.

How we build

Security is not an add-on

Ticket prices, payment status and ticket validity are always decided by our servers - never by the browser. Payments are processed by Paystack, Pesapal or PayHero and confirmed through verified provider webhooks. Ticket QR codes contain only a random ticket identifier, never personal data.

We store the minimum data needed to issue a ticket and admit an attendee, and we never keep card details on our platform.

Built on

Modern, scalable infrastructure

  • Cloudflare Workers for server-side logic at the edge
  • Cloudflare D1 for ticketing data
  • Cloudflare R2 for event posters and images
  • Firebase Authentication for organizer and staff access
  • Paystack for card, M-Pesa and mobile money payments
  • Cloudflare Turnstile for bot protection
