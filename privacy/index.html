Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Prince Alex TicketHub, an event ticketing and management platform operated by Prince Alex Digital ("we", "us", "our" or "the platform"), collects, uses, stores, shares and protects personal information.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling personal data responsibly in accordance with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, and other applicable data protection laws.

By using Prince Alex TicketHub, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Personal Data We Collect

We collect only the information reasonably necessary to operate the platform, provide our services, process transactions, support event organizers and protect users.

1.1 Ticket Buyers and Attendees

When you browse events, purchase tickets or claim free tickets, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Event registration details
  • Ticket type, quantity and unique ticket identifiers
  • Order references and transaction status
  • Ticket purchase, claim and check-in history
  • Communication and support requests relating to your order or event

The information collected may vary depending on the event's registration requirements.

1.2 Event Organizers and Event Staff

When you register as an organizer or event staff member, we may collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Account login and authentication information
  • Business or organization name
  • Business email address and phone number
  • Business logo and other profile information
  • Event details, configurations and management records
  • Payment provider configuration and relevant account references
  • Payout details where required for settlements
  • Event sales, attendee, check-in and reporting information

Authentication information is handled through secure authentication services. We do not intentionally store plaintext passwords.

1.3 Technical and Device Information

When you access TicketHub, we may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • IP address
  • Browser type and version
  • Device and operating system information, where available
  • Request timestamps
  • Access and activity logs
  • Basic diagnostic and error information
  • Security-related information used to detect suspicious activity

This information helps us maintain the platform's security, reliability and performance.

2. How We Use Your Personal Data

We use personal information for legitimate operational purposes, including:

  • Creating and managing ticket orders and free ticket claims.
  • Issuing tickets and sending order confirmations and receipts.
  • Verifying payment transactions and updating payment statuses.
  • Validating tickets and managing event check-ins.
  • Providing organizers with attendee lists, sales reports and event management tools for their own events.
  • Managing organizer accounts, staff permissions and business profiles.
  • Processing organizer settlements and reconciling transactions where applicable.
  • Responding to customer support requests and resolving payment or ticket-related issues.
  • Sending transactional notifications, such as ticket confirmations, receipts, event updates and important service announcements.
  • Detecting, preventing and investigating fraud, abuse, unauthorized access and suspicious transactions.
  • Maintaining platform security, troubleshooting technical problems and improving service reliability.
  • Complying with applicable legal, regulatory, tax and accounting obligations.
  • Enforcing our Terms of Service and protecting the rights, safety and legitimate interests of users and the platform.

Where consent is required by law, we will obtain it before processing personal data for the relevant purpose.

We do not use personal information for unrelated purposes without an appropriate lawful basis.

3. Payments and Payment Information

Prince Alex TicketHub supports multiple payment collection methods to give organizers flexibility in managing event payments.

Depending on the event configuration, payments may be processed through:

  • Paystack
  • PayHero
  • Pesapal
  • Platform Collection Mode, using payment infrastructure configured and managed by Prince Alex Digital.

3.1 Third-Party Payment Providers

When an organizer selects Paystack, PayHero or Pesapal, the selected provider processes payments through its own payment infrastructure.

Depending on the payment method, the provider may collect payment information such as card details, mobile money information or other details necessary to complete a transaction.

These providers process personal and payment data under their own privacy policies, terms and applicable legal obligations.

TicketHub may receive relevant transaction information, including:

  • Payment reference or transaction identifier
  • Order reference
  • Payment amount and currency
  • Payment method or provider
  • Transaction status
  • Transaction timestamps
  • Relevant refund, reversal or settlement status

This information is used to verify payments, issue tickets, reconcile transactions and support customers and organizers.

We do not intentionally store full payment card details, M-PESA PINs or other sensitive payment authentication credentials on TicketHub.

3.2 Platform Collection Mode

When an organizer selects Platform Collection Mode, payments are collected using the payment infrastructure configured by Prince Alex Digital.

In this mode, we may process relevant payment and transaction information to:

  • Confirm successful payments.
  • Associate payments with the correct event and order.
  • Issue or activate tickets.
  • Maintain transaction records.
  • Reconcile collected payments.
  • Calculate applicable platform fees.
  • Facilitate organizer settlements and approved refunds.
  • Investigate disputed, reversed or suspicious transactions.

Payment information is processed only to the extent necessary for these purposes and in accordance with applicable law.

3.3 Payment Security

Payment processing is subject to the security measures and requirements of the relevant payment provider and the platform's configured infrastructure.

Payment status is verified by our server-side systems rather than relying solely on information supplied by a customer's browser.

Customers should never share their payment PINs, passwords or authentication credentials with event organizers or platform support staff.

4. How We Share Personal Data

We do not sell personal data.

However, we may share personal information with trusted parties where necessary to operate TicketHub, fulfil transactions, deliver events, comply with legal obligations or protect users.

4.1 Event Organizers

When you purchase or claim a ticket, relevant attendee information may be shared with the organizer of that event.

This may include:

  • Attendee name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, where collected
  • Ticket type and quantity
  • Order or ticket reference
  • Ticket status and check-in information
  • Other registration information required for the event

Organizers use this information to manage attendee registration, communicate event-related information, verify tickets and facilitate entry.

Organizers are responsible for handling attendee information appropriately and in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

4.2 Payment Providers

Where applicable, relevant payment and transaction information is shared with Paystack, PayHero, Pesapal or the payment services used for Platform Collection Mode.

This sharing is necessary to process transactions, confirm payments, manage refunds, reconcile payments and prevent fraud.

The information shared depends on the selected payment method and provider requirements.

4.3 Technology and Infrastructure Providers

We use trusted third-party service providers to host, secure, operate and maintain TicketHub.

These may include providers of:

  • Cloud hosting, storage and content delivery
  • Authentication and account management
  • Payment processing and transaction verification
  • Email and communication services
  • Security monitoring and technical support
  • Application maintenance and other operational services

These providers may process personal data on our behalf or independently, depending on the services they provide and their legal arrangements.

We take reasonable steps to ensure that service providers handling personal data are subject to appropriate contractual, confidentiality and security requirements.

4.4 Legal and Regulatory Authorities

We may disclose personal information where required or permitted by applicable law, a valid court order, a lawful request from a competent authority or where disclosure is necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

4.5 Business Transfers

If Prince Alex Digital undergoes a merger, restructuring, acquisition, business transfer or similar transaction involving TicketHub, personal data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable data protection requirements.

5. Data Storage and Retention

We store and process personal information using secure cloud-based infrastructure and trusted technology service providers to operate and maintain Prince Alex TicketHub.

Personal data may be stored on secure servers or processed through third-party services that support our platform, authentication, payment processing, communications and security.

We retain personal data only for as long as reasonably necessary to:

  • Operate and maintain the platform.
  • Provide organizers with relevant event records.
  • Maintain ticket, order and transaction histories.
  • Resolve customer support requests, payment disputes and refund claims.
  • Meet applicable legal, regulatory, tax and accounting obligations.
  • Prevent fraud and protect the platform.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of data, event requirements, applicable laws and legitimate operational needs.

When personal data is no longer required, we take reasonable steps to securely delete, anonymize or otherwise dispose of it, subject to applicable legal retention requirements.

Where personal data is stored or processed outside Kenya, we take appropriate measures to comply with applicable data protection laws governing international data transfers.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, destruction or misuse.

Our security measures may include:

  • Encryption of data in transit using secure communication protocols.
  • Server-side handling of sensitive application logic and payment verification.
  • Secure management of application secrets and credentials.
  • Role-based access controls for organizer, staff and administrative functions.
  • Restricted access to sensitive operational information.
  • Secure authentication and account management.
  • Unique ticket identifiers to support ticket verification.
  • Security monitoring, error logging and investigation of suspicious activity.
  • Reasonable infrastructure and application security practices.

Ticket QR codes are designed to contain a random ticket identifier rather than directly exposing personal information such as names, email addresses or phone numbers.

While we take reasonable measures to protect data, no internet-based service or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

If a personal data breach occurs, we will take appropriate steps to investigate, contain and address the incident and notify affected individuals and relevant authorities where required by applicable law.

7. Your Data Protection Rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, and other applicable laws, you may have the right to:

  • Access: Request confirmation of whether we process your personal data and obtain access to relevant information.
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate, incomplete or outdated personal data.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of personal data where legally permissible.
  • Object: Object to certain forms of personal data processing, subject to applicable legal requirements.
  • Restriction: Request restriction of processing in circumstances provided for by law.
  • Withdraw consent: Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.
  • Data portability: Request a copy of your personal data in a structured, commonly used and machine-readable format where the legal conditions for portability apply.
  • Complain: Lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya if you believe your personal data has been handled unlawfully.

These rights are subject to applicable legal conditions and limitations. For example, we may need to retain certain transaction or accounting records even where deletion is requested.

7.1 How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise your data protection rights, submit a request to:

Email: support@princealex.digital

Please provide enough information for us to identify your relevant account, order or event records. We may request reasonable additional information to verify your identity before processing a request.

We will respond within the timeframes required by applicable law.

Where a request cannot be fully granted, we will explain the relevant legal or operational reasons where appropriate.

If you are dissatisfied with our response, you may lodge a complaint with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

8. Cookies and Local Storage

TicketHub may use browser storage and similar technologies to support the functionality, security and usability of the platform.

These may include:

  • Local storage for non-sensitive user interface preferences.
  • Authentication-managed session mechanisms.
  • Technical storage required for platform functionality and security.
  • Other technologies necessary to maintain reliable service operation.

We do not intentionally store payment secrets, full card details or M-PESA PINs in browser storage.

Where cookies or similar technologies are used for purposes requiring consent under applicable law, we will seek consent as required.

Users can manage browser storage and cookie settings through their browser. Disabling certain storage mechanisms may affect some platform features.

9. Children and Minors

Prince Alex TicketHub is not intended for children under 18 to independently create organizer accounts, enter into payment arrangements or use services where parental or guardian authorization is legally required.

Where an event is suitable for children, ticket purchases and registrations should be completed by a parent, guardian or another legally authorized adult, where appropriate.

Event organizers are responsible for communicating age restrictions, supervision requirements and other event-specific conditions.

Where we become aware that personal data has been collected from a child in circumstances that require parental or guardian consent and that consent has not been obtained, we will take appropriate steps in accordance with applicable law.

10. Data Processing by Event Organizers

Event organizers may receive attendee information through their event dashboards and related platform features.

Organizers are responsible for:

  • Using attendee data only for legitimate event-related purposes and other purposes permitted by law.
  • Providing appropriate privacy information to attendees where required.
  • Maintaining reasonable security measures for any attendee data they access, export or store.
  • Limiting access to attendee information to authorized personnel.
  • Not selling, unlawfully disclosing or misusing attendee information.
  • Complying with applicable data protection obligations, including requests from attendees where those obligations apply to the organizer.

Organizers must not use attendee information for unrelated marketing, promotional campaigns or other purposes without an appropriate lawful basis and any consent required by law.

Prince Alex Digital is responsible for its own processing activities as a platform operator and for fulfilling its applicable data protection obligations.

11. Marketing and Communications

We may send essential service communications relating to your use of TicketHub, including:

  • Ticket confirmations and electronic tickets.
  • Payment confirmations and receipts.
  • Event updates, postponements and cancellations.
  • Important account and security notifications.
  • Responses to customer support enquiries.
  • Other communications necessary to deliver the services.

Where we intend to send promotional or marketing communications, we will comply with applicable consent, notice and opt-out requirements.

You may opt out of marketing communications where an opt-out mechanism is provided. Opting out of marketing messages will not prevent us from sending essential transactional, security or legally required communications.

Event organizers are independently responsible for ensuring that their communications to attendees comply with applicable data protection and marketing laws.

12. Third-Party Websites and Services

TicketHub may contain links to external websites, payment pages, event pages or other third-party services.

These services may have their own privacy policies, terms and data processing practices.

When you access a third-party service, your personal information may be processed according to that service's policies and applicable law.

We encourage users to review the privacy policies of third-party services before submitting personal information to them.

Prince Alex Digital does not control the privacy practices of independent third parties, except to the extent required by our contractual or legal responsibilities.

13. International Data Transfers

Some of the technology and payment service providers used by TicketHub may process or store personal information in countries outside Kenya.

Where personal data is transferred outside Kenya, we will take appropriate steps to ensure that such transfers comply with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, and applicable regulations.

These steps may include appropriate safeguards, contractual protections, assessments or other legally recognized transfer mechanisms.

14. Data Breaches and Incident Management

We maintain reasonable procedures to identify, assess, respond to and manage personal data breaches and security incidents.

Where a breach occurs, we may:

  • Investigate the nature and extent of the incident.
  • Take steps to contain and mitigate its effects.
  • Restore affected services and strengthen security measures.
  • Notify the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner and affected individuals within the applicable statutory timeframes where required.
  • Cooperate with relevant authorities and take any other steps required by law.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, payment integrations, infrastructure, data processing practices or applicable laws.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Where material changes are made, we will take reasonable steps to notify affected users where appropriate or where required by law.

We encourage users to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about how their personal data is handled.
